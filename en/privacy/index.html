Privacy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Who we are

Youmechef is a meal service in Utrecht. Questions about privacy? Mail us.

What we collect

On this website only your email address, when you join the waitlist. This site uses no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers.

What we use it for

We mail you when your invite is ready and send an occasional launch update. We never sell your data.

Who processes it

  • FormSubmit delivers the waitlist form to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and sees your IP address, like any web host.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or the waitlist closes. After that we delete your address.

Your rights

You can view, correct, or delete your data. Mail us and we will sort it out.

The app

The Youmechef app has its own privacy statement, shown when you create an account.

Changes

We may update this statement. The date above shows when that last happened.
