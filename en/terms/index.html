About Youmechef

Youmechef is a meal service in Utrecht, currently in beta. You pick recipes and we weigh every ingredient exactly.

Ordering

You pay per order. There is no subscription and no minimum. An order is final once you have paid.

Prices

Portions start at €3,50. You always see the total before you pay.

Pickup

You collect your order at De Pers in Utrecht, at the time you choose. Can't make it? Mail us as soon as possible.

Fresh produce

We work with fresh, perishable produce weighed out just for you. A prepared order can therefore not be withdrawn. Something wrong with your order? Mail us and we will fix it.

Allergens

Recipes may contain allergens. Always check the labels of the delivered products.

Liability

Our liability is limited to the amount of your order, except where the law says otherwise.
