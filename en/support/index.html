Support

Questions? Mail us at hello@youmechef.com. We reply quickly.

How does ordering work?

Pick recipes in the app and set how many people you cook for per recipe. We weigh every ingredient exactly at De Pers in Utrecht. You pay per order, no subscription.

When can I collect or get delivery?

On Mondays and Fridays. Pickup is at De Pers in Utrecht. Bike delivery currently covers the Wittevrouwen neighbourhood.

What does it cost?

Portions start at €3,50. You always see the total before you pay.

Can I send in my own recipe?

Yes. Share a recipe from Instagram, snap a photo, or type it out. We read it, match every ingredient, and price it.

Something is wrong with my order

Sorry about that. Mail us at hello@youmechef.com and we will fix it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, or via this page.

Where is Youmechef available?

Youmechef is in beta in Utrecht. We are expanding step by step.
